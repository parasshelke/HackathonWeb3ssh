# 🌿 GreenChain

## Decentralized Carbon Offset Marketplace

GreenChain is a **Web3-based platform** that enables individuals, organizations, and environmental groups to record, verify, and monetize their carbon offset activities such as **tree planting, renewable energy projects, and environmental conservation initiatives**.

The platform combines **blockchain technology, NFTs, and crypto payments** to create a transparent and decentralized ecosystem where climate-positive actions are rewarded and tracked permanently on the blockchain.

GreenChain aims to solve the problem of **lack of transparency and trust in carbon offset programs** by ensuring that every environmental contribution is **securely recorded, verified, and tokenized**.

---

# 🌍 Problem Statement

Climate change is one of the biggest global challenges. Many organizations claim to plant trees or offset carbon emissions, but:

• There is **no transparent proof** of these actions.
• Data is often **centralized and can be manipulated**.
• Contributors rarely receive **recognition or incentives**.
• Carbon offset markets lack **trust and traceability**.

Because of these issues, people hesitate to invest in carbon offset programs.

---

# 💡 Solution – GreenChain

GreenChain provides a **blockchain-powered marketplace** where environmental actions can be **verified, tokenized, and rewarded**.

When an organization performs an activity such as:

• Planting trees
• Protecting forests
• Renewable energy installation
• Waste recycling programs

they can **submit proof on GreenChain**.

After verification:

✔ The action is stored on the **blockchain**
✔ A **Carbon Offset NFT (C-NFT)** is minted
✔ Contributors receive **crypto rewards**
✔ Buyers can purchase verified carbon offsets

---

# ⚙️ How the Platform Works

### Step 1 – Connect Wallet

Users connect their crypto wallet such as:

• MetaMask
• WalletConnect

This enables secure identity verification using Web3.

---

### Step 2 – Submit Carbon Offset

Organizations submit details such as:

• Organization name
• Environmental activity
• Number of trees planted
• Location
• Proof (images, GPS data, documents)

Example:

```
Organization: GreenEarth NGO
Activity: Planted 500 trees
Location: Maharashtra
Date: March 2025
```

---

### Step 3 – Verification Process

The system verifies the submission through:

• Community voting (DAO governance)
• Trusted environmental validators
• AI verification (optional)

Once approved, the project becomes a **verified carbon offset record**.

---

### Step 4 – NFT Minting

After verification, the system mints a **Carbon Offset NFT (C-NFT)**.

Example NFT:

```
C-NFT #001
Project: Tree Plantation
Location: Maharashtra
Trees Planted: 500
Verified by: GreenChain DAO
```

This NFT acts as **digital proof of climate action**.

---

### Step 5 – Marketplace

These NFTs can be:

• Bought by companies to offset emissions
• Collected as environmental proof
• Traded in the marketplace

Companies can buy NFTs to **achieve carbon neutrality**.
